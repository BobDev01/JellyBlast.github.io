This Privacy Policy explains how Solo Indie Dev ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Jelly Blast, our Android puzzle game, including package soloindiedev.jellyblast.

A quick overview

Your game progress and preferences are saved on your device. Jelly Blast uses Google AdMob for advertising, which processes certain device and usage information. You do not need to create an account to play.

1. Saved progress and preferences

Jelly Blast stores your current board, available pieces, score, high score, combo, unlocked or selected skins, sound and animation preferences, and information needed to resume a run on your device. It also stores local run identifiers, completed-game counts, and rewarded-continue counts to apply the game's ad and continue limits.

We use these records to provide gameplay, restore your progress, remember your choices, unlock skins, and manage in-game rewards. The game does not upload these saved-game records to a server operated by Solo Indie Dev. It has no player-account registration or developer-operated cloud-save service.

2. Advertising and third-party services

Jelly Blast integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. The game supports interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads that offer an in-game continue.

Google's advertising SDK can collect and share:

  • IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostics about app and advertising performance.
  • Device or account-related identifiers, including advertising ID and app set ID where available.

Google uses this information for ad delivery and measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Processing depends on the SDK, device settings, region, and applicable privacy choices. Google and its advertising partners may process information on servers outside your country.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. Google's advertising SDK disclosure explains the data categories in more detail. Links to ads or external sites are governed by those services' policies.

3. Advertising and consent choices

Where applicable, Google's consent interface lets you make choices about advertising data. When Google requires an ongoing privacy-options entry point, a Privacy Options control is shown in the game's Settings menu so you can revisit those choices. The options displayed depend on your region and the messages configured for the game.

You can also use the advertising privacy controls available in your Android settings, including resetting or deleting your advertising ID where supported. These settings do not necessarily stop all advertising or all data processing. You can choose not to watch an optional rewarded ad and start a new game instead.

Where consent is required, the relevant advertising processing relies on that choice. Other processing may be needed to provide the game, respond to you, maintain service security, or meet legal obligations, as permitted by applicable law.

4. Information you send to support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or other details you choose to send. We use this information to answer questions, investigate problems, and respond to privacy requests. Please send only information relevant to your request. Email is handled by our email provider, Google.

5. Sharing and security

Advertising information is processed by Google and the advertising services described above. Support communications are processed through our email provider. We may disclose information we hold when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to investigate abuse or protect legal rights. We do not publish your email correspondence or sell your locally saved scores or progress.

Local save files use the app's storage on your device. Google states that data transmitted by its advertising SDK is encrypted using TLS. We use reasonable safeguards for information we handle, but no storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

6. Retention and deletion

Game records: saved progress and preferences remain on your device until they are overwritten through gameplay or removed. To delete the game's local records, use Android's app settings to clear Jelly Blast's storage, or uninstall the app. Clearing storage removes your high score, saved run, skins, and preferences.

Support messages: we keep correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request, maintain relevant support records, or meet applicable legal requirements. You can request deletion by emailing us.

Google's records: advertising and consent data are retained under Google's policies, including its data-retention policy. Clearing or uninstalling Jelly Blast does not automatically delete information already held by Google. Use Google's privacy controls or contact Google for requests concerning its records.

7. Children and teenagers

Jelly Blast is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you are below the age at which you can make your own data-protection decisions where you live, involve a parent or guardian before providing personal information or making privacy choices.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Solo Indie Dev, contact us so we can investigate and delete information under our control where appropriate.

8. Your privacy requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information we hold about you; object to or restrict processing; withdraw consent; or complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us using the email below. We may need to verify your request and will respond as required by applicable law.

Solo Indie Dev cannot remotely retrieve or erase save files stored only on your device. Use the device controls described above for those files. Requests about Google's own processing may also need to be made directly through Google's privacy tools.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date at the top. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent for relevant changes.
